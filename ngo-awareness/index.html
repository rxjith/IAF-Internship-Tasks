Get To Know Us Better

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization, licensed by the Central Government with its base in Chhattisgarh.

The foundation holds 80G & 12A certifications, ensuring transparency, accountability and tax-exempt benefits for donors. It is also CSR-1 registered, enabling collaborations with corporate partners for impactful CSR initiatives.

InAmigos Foundation is NITI Aayog registered and holds the prestigious IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification, reflecting its commitment to maintaining high-quality standards in operations and service delivery.

With a strong network of professionals, volunteers and corporate partners, the organization works tirelessly to bring positive change in society across India.
